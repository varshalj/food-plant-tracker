🌱 Welcome to PlantTrack!

Track 30 different plants per week for better gut health. Let's get you set up:

Create your Notion Database

Duplicate our template to your Notion workspace:

📋 Duplicate Notion Template
2

Get your Notion Token & DB ID

  1. Go to notion.so/my-integrations
  2. Click "New integration" → Name it "PlantTrack"
  3. Copy the "Internal Integration Token" (starts with secret_)
  4. Open your duplicated database in Notion
  5. Click ••• → "Connections" → Add your "PlantTrack" integration
  6. Copy the Database ID from the URL:
    notion.so/[DATABASE_ID]?v=...
3

Get your OpenAI API Key

  1. Go to platform.openai.com/api-keys
  2. Click "Create new secret key"
  3. Copy the key (starts with sk-)

💡 You'll need to add credits to your OpenAI account. ~$5 lasts months of usage.
