Connect PlantTrack to your Notion

This allows PlantTrack to save your plant entries.

Show me how
  1. Go to notion.so/my-integrations
  2. Click "New integration" → Name it "PlantTrack"
  3. Copy the "Internal Integration Token" (starts with secret_)
  4. Open your duplicated database in Notion
  5. Click ••• → "Connections" → Add "PlantTrack"
  6. Copy the Database ID from the URL:
    notion.so/[DATABASE_ID]?v=...

You can disconnect anytime from Notion settings.
